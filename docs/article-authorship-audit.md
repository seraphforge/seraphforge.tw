# Seraph Article Authorship Audit

Audit scope: current files under `source/_posts/`.

This audit does not determine whether any article was written by AI or by a human. It does not use AI detectors. It only records observable editorial signals: concrete environment details, commands or error messages, first-person experience, generic structure, and whether the author should reconfirm the article before publication.

## Status Legend

- `keep`: can remain public from an authorship-workflow perspective.
- `review`: author should confirm details or approve paragraphs.
- `rewrite`: author should draft again from original notes before publishing.
- `archive`: consider removing from public publication if the author cannot verify the core experience or claims.

## Overall Notes

- No current article under `source/_posts/` contains a complete reproducible technical environment with date, device, OS/version, commands, outputs, and error messages.
- The strongest first-person evidence appears in the personal reflection articles and the CTF/malware, retail, Codex, medical cybersecurity, and conference reflection posts.
- The technical or semi-technical posts should generally be treated as reflective essays unless the author later adds verified notes, screenshots, logs, commands, or environment details.
- Translation variants should be reviewed by the author because several articles make broad claims or express personal memories where tone matters.
- No article in this audit is marked `rewrite` or `archive` based only on current evidence. The recurring recommendation is `review` where factual, technical, workplace, or third-party details need confirmation.
