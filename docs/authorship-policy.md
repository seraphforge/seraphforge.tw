# Seraph Authorship Policy

This site treats the author's original draft as the source of truth.

## Core Rule

Every article must be built from the author's own words, experience, observations, arguments, and conclusions. AI-generated full articles must not be published under the author's name as if they were original human drafts.

## Acceptable AI Assistance

AI may help with:

- Finding typos.
- Finding unclear grammar.
- Pointing out confusing paragraphs.
- Reminding the author when environment details are missing.
- Organizing material the author has already written.
- Suggesting questions the author should answer before publication.

## Not Allowed

AI must not:

- Invent experiments.
- Invent error messages.
- Invent devices, models, versions, or environments.
- Invent interviews or conversations.
- Invent personal feelings or memories.
- Automatically generate the article's conclusion for the author.
- Rewrite a personal article into a polished voice that no longer reflects the author's own draft.

## Editing Workflow

When editing an article, AI should ask questions before rewriting. If a paragraph is unclear, the first response should identify what is missing or ambiguous.

Major rewrites must be confirmed by the author paragraph by paragraph. The author should decide which claims, examples, tone, and conclusions remain.

## Technical Articles

Technical articles should preserve concrete working context whenever possible:

- Experiment date.
- Device.
- Operating system and version.
- Commands used.
- Error messages.
- Failed attempts.
- The author's judgment at the time.
- Unresolved problems.

## Personal Viewpoint Articles

Personal viewpoint articles should start from the author's own draft. AI may suggest structure, missing context, or places where the argument is unclear, but it should not create the core personal experience or conclusion.

## Detection Claims

Do not add typos, filler, slang, or incorrect information to avoid AI detection. Also do not claim that any detector can reliably prove whether an article was written by a human or by AI.
